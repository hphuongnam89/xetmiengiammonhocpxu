# Phase 1 Risks

- The PDFs are scanned documents; OCR can misread Vietnamese text, dates, signatures, stamps, and tables.
- XLSX files contain student-named sheets, possible duplicates, formulas, blank cells, and mixed curriculum versions.
- A sheet count does not prove that every sheet is complete or approved.
- Historical spreadsheet results must not be treated as rules until they are validated against the governing documents.
- Human validation is required before importing rules, course mappings, or historical decisions into production data.
