# Phase 3 Risks

- A workbook may contain more than one curriculum version or a copied template.
- Formula cells may have stale cached values.
- Course names and codes may differ across versions without proving equivalence.
- Student sheets may resemble curriculum sheets but contain decisions rather than definitions.
- Missing dates must remain unknown rather than being inferred from filenames or file timestamps.
- A future importer must quarantine ambiguous rows for human review.
